ABOUT POCKETROLL

POCKETROLL 35 turns the phone in your pocket into a 35mm film camera that runs in your browser. It is built around the experience of shooting film — not a filter you apply to a photograph after it has already been taken.

The camera is five moves: choose a body, load a film stock, compose and expose one frame at a time, develop the roll to reveal the prints, keep them in the Film Archive.

Every stock carries its own colour science and its own grain; every body its own lens, colour cast, sharpness and quirks. Grain follows the ISO, light leaks follow the film. Nothing here is a preset you dial in.

No endless editing. No endless shooting. Just you, your camera, and one roll of film.

QUICK START

five steps · the first roll takes about two minutes
01
CHOOSE YOUR CAMERA
The camera-and-film mark right of the shutter opens CAMERA BAG. Every body is simulated separately: its lens, colour cast, sharpness, quirks.
02
LOAD YOUR FILM
Its left page is THE ROLL. Tap a stock to read its preview — samples rendered by this app itself, not photographs from the net — then LOAD FILM.
03
TAKE YOUR SHOTS
Press the red shutter. Tap the viewfinder for ISO, shutter and exposure value. Each shot takes one exposure off the counter.
04
DEVELOP YOUR ROLL
Exposed frames stay latent until the print is finished. THE ROLL offers DEVELOP ROLL when the roll is done, or earlier if you choose to stop.
05
VISIT YOUR ARCHIVE
The frame-grid mark left of the shutter opens FILM ARCHIVE: BY ROLLS, ALL FRAMES, LAB SCANS. Tap a frame to download it; open a roll to export it as a .zip.

KNOW YOUR CAMERA

what each part of the screen is for
VIEWFINDER
The LCD window: the frame exactly as the camera will print it. Tap it for the full readouts.
FRAMES LEFT
Exposures remaining on the roll in the camera: 36 on a 35mm body, less on a 120 body (8 for 6×7, 16 for 6×4.5). Reading 00 means finished, not broken.
CAMERA BAG
The square right of the shutter, its right page: the bodies this camera simulates. Choosing one changes the lens, the colour cast and the quirks — never the roll.
THE ROLL
The same square, its left page: the exposures left, DEVELOP ROLL, CONTACT SHEET, OPEN BACK DOOR, and the shelf of film stocks to thread.
ARCHIVE
The square left of the shutter: developed rolls, kept frames, imported scans, favorites, backup.
SHUTTER
The red circle in the middle. It exposes a frame; it never edits one. Spacebar works while no page is open.
SETTINGS
The header button: print options, camera recovery, Pro access, this manual.
THE SETTINGS PAGE · four switches · none of them changes your roll
DATE STAMP
An orange corner mark printed at the moment of exposure, the way a lab prints a stamp. Scanned-in photographs are stamped too.
ON-CAMERA FLASH
Only bodies with a built-in flash offer it. The centre lifts, highlights lose a little saturation, the background goes deeper.
FRONT SENSOR
The front camera, for self-portraits. Roll and film stay as they are.
RETRY CAMERA
If the browser blocked the sensor: allow camera access, then tap this.

A NOTE ABOUT FILM

A roll holds a fixed number of exposures: 36 on a 35mm body, fewer on a 120 body — 8 for 6×7, 16 for 6×4.5. Every photograph takes exactly one of them.

Nothing appears until you develop. Frames you have exposed stay latent in the strip: dark, numbered, carrying their edge marks. A tap gives a momentary peek, never a print.

DEVELOP ROLL runs the whole roll through developer, stop bath, fixer and wash. The photographs then appear in the Film Archive and a contact sheet is printed for that roll. It is irreversible: once a roll comes out, this camera threads a fresh one.

OPEN BACK DOOR before developing fogs every frame still latent in the camera. Those photographs are lost and the spent roll leaves. Changing film while frames are latent is refused for the same reason — develop the roll, or open the door. Nothing is discarded silently.

A photograph you scan in is a lab print, not an exposure: it stays on the scan shelf and never changes the frame count.

A roll is more than a collection of photographs. Take your time. Make every frame count.

IMPORTANT NOTES
Installation options vary with the browser, the operating system and how the app is configured. POCKETROLL 35 is a web application, not a native App Store or Google Play application. Installing it does not guarantee that photographs already held in your browser carry over to the installed version — export a backup from the Archive first.
